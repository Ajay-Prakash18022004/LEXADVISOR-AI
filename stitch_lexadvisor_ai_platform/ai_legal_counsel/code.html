Clause 8 of my lease agreement states that if I vacate before 11 months, my entire security deposit of ₹1,50,000 will be forfeited immediately without dispute. Is this legally enforceable in Hyderabad?

Grounding: High Court of Telangana • Supreme Court of India • Indian Contract Act
Executive Opinion

In short: No, this clause is legally vulnerable and largely unenforceable in its present absolute form.

Under Indian contract jurisprudence and Telangana tenancy precedents, landlords cannot levy arbitrary punitive damages without establishing actual financial loss incurred as a direct consequence of early termination.

Statutory Grounding & High Court Authorities
SECTION 74 Central Act

Indian Contract Act, 1872

Liquidated damages must represent a genuine pre-estimate of reasonable loss. Forfeiture of an entire substantial sum without proof of vacant period or financial damage constitutes an invalid in terrorem penalty.

Precedent Authority: Kailash Nath Associates v. DDA (2015) 4 SCC 136
SECTION 108(q) Property Law

Transfer of Property Act, 1882

Governs surrender of possession upon notice. When adequate contractual notice (typically 30 days) is tendered, the landlord is bound to adjust only actual utility dues or documented tenant damages.

Precedent Authority: Fateh Chand v. Balkishan Das (AIR 1963 SC 1405)
STATUTORY CEILING TS Tenancy

Telangana Model Tenancy Standard

Statutory guidance strictly caps security deposits for residential premises to a maximum of 2 months' rent. For your current rent of ₹35,000/mo, permissible statutory deposit is ₹70,000 (collected: ₹1,50,000).

Statutory Variance: ₹80,000 Excess Over Ceiling
warning

High Commercial & Self-Help Deduction Exposure

Action Required

While the clause is legally unenforceable in court, the landlord holds physical custody of the funds. If you unilaterally vacate without executing a formal legal notice, the lessor is likely to withhold the ₹1,50,000 under the pretext of Clause 8. Initiating recovery later in the Small Causes Court (Hyderabad) entails an average procedural timeline of 14-18 months.

check_circle Recommendation: Issue a formal Pre-Emptive Representation Notice 30 days prior to surrender.
