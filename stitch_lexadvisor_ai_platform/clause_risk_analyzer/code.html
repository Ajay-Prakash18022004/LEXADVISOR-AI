dangerous Critical Risk (9.4/10) Clause 8: Lock-in & Deposit Forfeiture • Section: Financial Terms & Security
1. Original Draft Language format_quote

“If tenant vacates premises prior to the expiry of 11 months, the entire security deposit of ₹1,50,000 shall stand immediately forfeited without any claim or dispute.”

Deemed Unfair Penalty • Indian Contract Act §74
2. Plain English Translation psychology

What does this mean for you?

If you have a sudden job transfer or personal emergency and need to leave after 6 months, the landlord will seize your entire ₹1,50,000 deposit even if you gave 2 months advance notice. Under Supreme Court precedents, pre-estimated liquidated damages without actual proof of loss are void.

info Financial Exposure Risk: ₹1,50,000
3. Statutorily Balanced Counter-Clause verified

“Either party may terminate this agreement by providing two (2) months prior written notice. Upon vacating and handing over peaceful possession, the security deposit shall be refunded in full after deducting only pending utility bills and mutually documented pre-agreed painting charges.”

Violates Peaceful Possession • T.P. Act §108(m)

Privacy Infringement Risk

The landlord, property managers, or real estate brokers can enter your private home unannounced whenever they want without asking you first, compromising your constitutional privacy and quiet enjoyment.

1. Original Draft Language format_quote

Usurious Rate • Unilateral Arbitrator Void (Perkins Eastman)

Extortionate Penalty & Biased Forum

Late rent compounds exponentially (over 200% per annum!), and you lose the right to an independent arbitrator. The Supreme Court strictly invalidates agreements where one party exclusively appoints the sole arbitrator.
