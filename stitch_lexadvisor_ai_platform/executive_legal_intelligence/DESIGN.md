## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, institutional-grade digital interface tailored for legal advisory, statutory document intelligence, and compliance telemetry. Operating in the regional jurisdiction of Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, the design must bridge high-stakes judicial validity with modern, low-friction software engineering.

The brand personality projects:
- **Judicial Gravitas:** Unshakable stability, institutional credibility, and statutory precision.
- **Cognitive Clarity:** Translating dense civil, commercial, and property codes into actionable telemetry without patronizing simplification.
- **Executive Elegance:** A disciplined, high-density aesthetic reminiscent of premium global SaaS dashboards blended with the solemnity of constitutional chambers.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate Modern** with **Architectural Restraint**:
- Pristine, daylight-balanced backdrops (`#F8FAFC`) with razor-sharp slate borders (`#E2E8F0`).
- Deep midnight navy foundations anchor high-priority navigational structural zones.
- Selective warm gold highlights evoke judicial seals and premium verifications, while precision-engineered telemetry colors (emerald, amber, ruby, and interactive cobalt) communicate regulatory risk postures instantaneously.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for prolonged analytical sessions requiring rigorous document review, clause inspection, and dispute assessment.

### Core Roles
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`):** The clean, neutral background surface minimizing eye fatigue across long document reading sessions.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white container surfaces providing sharp, high-contrast separation from the base canvas.
- **Primary Navy (`#0F172A`):** The dominant authority color, applied to primary typography, master navigation sidebars, and critical action buttons.
- **Secondary Navy (`#1E293B`):** Structural sub-framing, table headers, active state panels, and card headers.
- **Accent Gold (`#D97706`):** Reserved for statutory authenticity, verification badges, stamp duty indicators, and formal certification seals.
- **Interactive Blue (`#2563EB`):** Dedicated exclusively to actionable interfaces, dynamic links, focus outlines, and primary workflow progress states.
- **Muted Slate (`#64748B`):** Secondary metadata, breadcrumbs, timestamp markers, and deactivated indicator strokes.
- **Structural Border (`#E2E8F0`):** Precise 1px boundary lines enforcing strict typographic and component alignment.

### Telemetry & Risk Indicators
- **Success Emerald (`#059669`):** Clause cleared, verified title, low litigation risk, active lease validity.
- **Warning Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Ambiguous clauses, stamp duty shortfall, upcoming statutory limitation deadline.
- **Danger Ruby (`#DC2626`):** Title encumbrance, critical non-compliance, statutory violation, penalty risk.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy is calibrated for analytical density and statutory authority:

- **Headlines & Display (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provides an executive, contemporary presence. Used for page titles, summary card headers, metric groupings, and module headers. Its geometric structure feels modern while retaining dignified restraint.
- **Body & Telemetry (Inter):** Serves all legal prose, clauses, analytics, tabular ledgers, and interactive controls. Its neutral grotesk architecture guarantees legibility across long-form bilingual translations, property schedule numbers, and dense multi-column grids.
- **Statutory Accents:** For formal certificates, digitally signed verification blocks, stamp duty affirmations, or high-tier jurisdictional seals, typographic layouts may incorporate uppercase letter-spacing styling via `label-caps` to emulate the formal cadence of high-court gazettes.

## Layout & Spacing

The system implements a rigid 12-column fluid grid system paired with strict 4px/8px incremental spatial rhythm.

### Grid & Margins
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid, `margin: 2rem` (32px), `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px). Maximum container width is capped at 1600px for multi-pane split workflows (e.g., document viewer on left, AI analysis ledger on right).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid, `margin: 1.5rem` (24px), `gutter: 1rem` (16px). Secondary toolbars collapse into segmented trays.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin: 1rem` (16px), `gutter: 0.75rem` (12px). Multi-column comparison charts collapse into tabbed carousels or stacked accordions.

### Spatial Rhythm
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro-alignment between icons and label copy, status dot clearances.
- `space-sm` (8px): Gaps between badge tags, inner padding of dense data cells.
- `space-md` (16px): Internal padding for secondary modules, form field gaps, button padding.
- `space-lg` (24px): Standard card container padding, section gaps within modals.
- `space-xl` (32px): Separation between master grid rows and analytical summary widgets.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, excessive skeuomorphism, or blurry glass surfaces. Depth is established through **architectural stratification** and **crisp planar boundaries**:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` flat surface. Holds navigational sidebars and page backgrounds.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** `#FFFFFF` background with a crisp border of `1px solid #E2E8F0` and an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Elevated Cards / Flyouts):** Used when hovering over actionable legal briefs or when opening interactive clause breakdowns. Styled with `1px solid #CBD5E1` and shadow `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Command Palettes):** `#FFFFFF` surface with an assertive border `1px solid #94A3B8` and shadow `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a 40% opacity navy backdrop (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`).
- **Telemetry Insets:** Document reading viewports and dense code/statute quote blocks utilize an inset background of `#F1F5F9` with a subtle inner border `1px solid #E2E8F0` to clearly delineate verbatim legal text from advisory commentary.

## Shapes

The design balances precision and approachable digital ergonomics:

- **Cards & Data Panels:** Formed with `0.75rem` to `1rem` (12px to 16px) radii (`rounded-lg`), providing an executive finish that softens complex tabular data without feeling informal.
- **Buttons & Input Fields:** Standardized at `0.5rem` (8px, base roundedness) to preserve an intentional, sharp tool-like utility.
- **Badges, Tags, and Pill Counters:** Set to fully rounded circular caps (`9999px`) to create clear geometric contrast against rectangular data tables and cards.
- **Checkboxes:** Micro-radii of `0.25rem` (4px).
- **Risk Score Gauges:** Clean radial or semi-circular vector arcs bound to strict 8px stroke profiles with rounded terminals.

  - *Statutory Seal:* Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#78350F`, border `1px solid #FCD34D`, with subtle `#D97706` icon accent.

### Input Fields & Search Controls
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, border-radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.625rem 0.875rem`, text `#0F172A`. Placeholder text `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: border-color `#2563EB`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)`.
- Input fields accompanied by jurisdiction dropdown selectors (e.g., "GHMC - Hyderabad", "APCRDA - Amaravati") utilize inset leading icon markers.

### Cards & Analytical Modules
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, border-radius `0.75rem` or `1rem`, padding `1.5rem`.
- Card Header: Features a clean horizontal layout with a `title-md` title, secondary metadata badge, and contextual overflow action menu. Dividers within cards are single-line `#F1F5F9` rules.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `1.125rem` x `1.125rem`, border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, border-radius `0.25rem`.
- Checked state: background `#2563EB`, border `#2563EB`, white checkmark glyph.

### Specialized LegalTech Components
- **Risk Score Gauge:** A calibrated circular or radial arc visualization presenting scores from 0 to 100. Incorporates numeric score in `metric-number` typography, flanked by an analytical risk tier badge (Low, Moderate, Severe).
- **Clause Audit Split-View:** Dual-column reading layout with synced scroll behavior. The statutory source document rests on an inset `#F8FAFC` viewport; AI annotations, counter-clauses, and case law citations occupy the right card rail.
- **Legal Action Steps (Stepper):** Numbered sequence component mapping out statutory legal recourse (e.g., Legal Notice -> Rejoinder -> RERA/Consumer Forum Filing), highlighting deadlines computed against local limitation acts.
